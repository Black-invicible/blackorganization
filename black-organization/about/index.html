About Black Organization

Black Organization is a Lagos-based creative and technology enterprise founded and led by CT Black. It brings several subsidiaries together under one name, with work moving across software, graphic design, video editing, writing, AI and digital media.

Our focus is telling Nigerian and African stories through original, locally rooted creative work.

Subsidiaries

  • Designwurld: graphic design
  • OworldTech: software development
  • Creative Writing Academy: writing and training
  • BlackWears: fashion
  • Golden Empire International and Golden Empire Studio
  • Gem Wash Laundry: everyday services
  • Grace Nation International: ministry
